## 2. Treasury: provenance, schema and as-of controls

- **Official family**: `daily_treasury_yield_curve` is the Treasury daily **nominal par yield curve**, not a real/TIPS curve, Treasury bill rates, FRBNY original bid quotes, the Board H.15 release or FRED distribution.
- **Documented entry fields**: `d:NEW_DATE` (observation date), `d:BC_2YEAR` and `d:BC_10YEAR` (`Edm.Double` in Treasury's example). Each is an XML *field*, not proof that the project-internal `daily_treasury_yield_curve:2YR` or `:10YR` is a genuine publisher-native series ID. The machine-readable matrix preserves `publisher_native_series_key = null` for both maturities.
- **Direct feed**: official documentation describes the `/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/pages/xml` endpoint with `data=daily_treasury_yield_curve`, `field_tdr_date_value=YYYY` or `field_tdr_date_value_month=YYYYMM`. The special `all` selector is paginated from zero, with feed handling of missing elements described in the developer notice. No endpoint request was made; pagination and missing-null handling should be tested with invented fixtures only.
- **Revision/vintage semantics**: the XML's `NEW_DATE` is the **data/observation date**, not proof of public release time, first seen, source revision ID, or retention right. The sample's `<updated>` timestamps are publisher example markup, **not** evidence of any actual live release. Normal Treasury availability is generally by **18:00 Eastern, sometimes later**, according to its [methodology page](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/interest-rate-statistics/treasury-yield-curve-methodology). Friday 22:00 UTC cannot always include the latest Friday 2Y/10Y observation.
- **One coherent curve vintage**: 2Y and 10Y must share a specific immutable same-publisher curve snapshot/version. A metadata field match or same observation date is insufficient for look-ahead-free historical parity.
- **Independent rights**: [Data.gov catalog's Treasury CC0 metadata](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/interest-rate-statistics-daily-treasury-yield-curve-rates) is positive evidence, not clearance of underlying indicative quote conditions, transport, calculation, long-lived logs/caches, backups, public aggregates, AI/ML or corrections. Treasury, H.15 and FRED remain three different source/transport contexts.

## 3. EIA: three different stock identities and automation

- `WCESTUS1`: EIA weekly U.S. ending crude stocks **excluding SPR**; series title and thousand-barrel units are documented. It is an inventory level rather than a futures or spot price.
- `PET_STOC_WSTK_A_EPC0_SAX_MBBL_W`: EIA commercial crude stock table explicitly **excluding lease stock**; the [EIA weekly stock definitions](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/TblDefs/pet_sum_sndw_tbldef.asp) describe crude-stock locations, end-of-period measurement and non-SPR/SPR distinctions.
- `PET_STOC_WSTK_A_EPC0_SAXL_MBBL_W`: separate EIA historical commercial crude stock category **including lease stock**; EIA's current summary exposes the category only through 2016. Its presence and a separate January 2005 **total-stock** exclusion note reveal definitional-change risks; neither implies when `WCESTUS1` changed nor permits stitching of the two.
- The publisher's current WPSR page includes formats distributed at 10:30 and 13:00 Eastern, so a **format-specific** actual public first-seen time and any changes/corrections must be observed and documented **only after separately approved rights**.
- EIA [copyrights and reuse](https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php) support assessment of EIA-created statistical survey information but exclude protected third-party inputs; survey inventory permission would **not** clear independently licensed WTI/Brent spot data. The [automated retrieval policy](https://www.eia.gov/about/privacy_security_policy.php) also restricts abusive automation and may block robots lacking contact information; neither policy is a signed authorization for this project.
- The [EIA WPSR page](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/) exposes CSV/JSON distributions in its product interface. This does **not** verify the exact historical `WCESTUS1` API route, version archival contract or allowed polling pattern.
